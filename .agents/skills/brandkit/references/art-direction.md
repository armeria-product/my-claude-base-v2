# Brandkit art direction

## Strategy becomes a visual argument

Start with category, audience, product function, emotional promise, cultural
position, trust level, visual world, metaphor, and explicit avoidances. A
developer tool can use building, precision, frames, scaffolds, or cursors; a
security product can use boundary, vigilance, protected core, or watch logic.
Those are starting points, not logo recipes.

Choose one logo method, or at most two when they reinforce one another:

- monogram plus a meaningful metaphor;
- a reduced product action;
- subtle metaphor fusion;
- crisp negative space;
- construction geometry made from circles, cuts, grids, frames, or modules.

The mark must be simple, balanced, memorable, scalable, and consistent across
icon, wordmark, badge, UI application, and pattern. Avoid unmotivated bolts,
animals, crests, clip art, random sparkles, and copied famous marks.

## Board systems

The default 3 by 3 overview has these nine jobs:

1. logo cover with calm negative space;
2. logo construction or reduction logic;
3. digital application such as browser chrome, an app header, or terminal;
4. one short brand essence statement;
5. color system;
6. type specimen or hierarchy;
7. physical application;
8. atmospheric image direction;
9. UI, badge, icon, input, or pattern detail.

For a compact 2 by 3 mini-deck, use logo/wordmark, product surface, functional
panel, atmosphere, construction or badge, and a quiet system promise. Vary
quiet, functional, emotional, technical, atmospheric, and detailed panels so
the board has rhythm.

## Select a visual world

Keep one mode and palette coherent across every panel.

- Dark builder: near-black, precise grids, mono accents, controlled cyan/coral/
  lime, terminal or frame logic.
- Dark operator: black, red or amber restraint, flows, chips, and tactical
  product surfaces.
- Dark calm system: deep green, fog, horizon/path imagery, and quiet editorial
  spacing.
- Dark security: black or navy, radar/boundary cues, controlled alert accents.
- Light editorial trust: warm ivory, paper, small serif labels, deep blue/red/
  gold restraint.
- Luxury/editorial: stone, espresso, emboss, refined monogram, and material
  crops.
- Voice/communication: indigo or lilac accents, wave/pulse/speech logic.
- Cultural/experimental: print, halftone, CRT, or poster energy with disciplined
  hierarchy.

Use a base, primary accent, secondary accent, and neutral scale. Accents repeat
across panels; avoid random rainbow and unjustified purple-blue AI glow.

## Image, copy, and application restraint

Use cinematic or product-relevant imagery, paper/material texture, architecture,
or controlled abstract systems. Use believable browser chrome, terminal,
app-icon, label, card, folder, badge, or packaging applications. They are
identity proof, not full fictional dashboards.

Text is sparse and readable: a brand name, one short tagline, a URL/command,
and a few labels are enough. Avoid long paragraphs, lorem ipsum, dense menus,
tiny fake UI, generic corporate slogans, or buzzword soup.

With references, reproduce the rhythm, grid, spacing, scale, density, image
treatment, and color behavior without copying protected brand content.
